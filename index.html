Reliable and Certifiable Human-Centred Agentic AI for Structural Systems

A European network advancing trustworthy agentic AI across the full structural-engineering lifecycle — from the design of new structures to the identification, assessment, monitoring and retrofit of existing systems.

Express your interest ↗ Read the concept note

The engineer remains the responsible decision-maker. RELIANCE focuses on reliability, verification, uncertainty and meaningful human oversight.

RELIANCE
The vision

AI agents that extend engineering capability without displacing engineering responsibility.

RELIANCE brings together structural engineers, computational scientists, AI researchers, monitoring specialists, reliability experts and practitioners to establish how agentic systems can be used credibly in safety-critical structural engineering.

The objective is not autonomous engineering without oversight. It is a verifiable human–AI workflow in which agents can plan analyses, orchestrate engineering tools, seek additional evidence, quantify uncertainty and document reasoning — while qualified engineers retain authority over objectives, assumptions, escalation and final decisions.

Two complementary engineering missions

New structures and existing structures, connected by one trustworthy agentic framework.

01

Design of new structures

Agent-supported exploration and verification of structural concepts, numerical models, design alternatives and code-compliant solutions.

  • Computational mechanics and optimisation
  • Engineering software and solver orchestration
  • Reliability-based and uncertainty-aware design
  • Automated checks with traceable human review
02

Assessment & retrofit of existing structures

Agentic reasoning connected to real structural evidence — from sensing and inspection to identification, safety assessment and retrofit decisions.

  • Structural identification and model updating
  • SHM, NDT, UAVs, robotics and digital twins
  • Residual capacity, risk and decision support
  • Retrofit planning and intervention strategies
A trustworthy engineering loop

Reliability, safety and human oversight are not add-ons — they are the core scientific problem.

1 Engineer defines objective Requirements · constraints · acceptance criteria
→
2 Agent plans & orchestrates Models · tools · analyses · evidence requests
→
3 Evidence is generated Simulation · sensing · inspection · data
→
4 Reliability is assessed UQ · V&V · robustness · failure modes
→
5 Human reviews & decides Challenge · override · escalate · accept
01

Compare against human practice

Benchmark agent-supported workflows against conventional engineering and, where scientifically useful, agent-only baselines.

02

Make uncertainty visible

Calibrate confidence, expose epistemic gaps and require additional evidence when the basis for a decision is insufficient.

03

Keep authority explicit

Define which decisions require professional review, independent checks or escalation, and document who remains accountable.

Working Groups

Four WGs connecting scientific foundations to engineering uptake.

WG1

Trust, reliability, verification & human oversight

Reliability metrics, uncertainty, verification and validation, failure modes, trust calibration, human authority and escalation.

WG2

Structural design, identification, assessment & retrofit

New structural design, computational mechanics, existing-system identification, safety assessment, residual capacity and retrofit.

WG3

Autonomous inspection, monitoring & cyber-physical interaction

SHM, digital twins, sensing, NDT, UAVs, robotic inspection and agent-planned evidence acquisition.

WG4

Standards, education & uptake

Training Schools, reusable educational resources, standards interfaces, stakeholder engagement and open knowledge.

A network that trains the next generation

Training Schools are a core RELIANCE output.

RELIANCE will build reusable teaching material and hands-on training around agentic structural design, existing-system assessment, monitoring, digital twins, reliability, verification and responsible human–AI engineering.
